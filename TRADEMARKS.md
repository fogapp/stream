# Trademarks

The code of Fog and its apps is open source. Their names and logos are not.
This policy is the same in every repository under
[github.com/fogapp](https://github.com/fogapp): Fog, Fin, Stream, Glass and
the catalogs.

The licenses (the GNU AGPL for the apps, MIT for the client libraries and
catalogs) cover copyright. They grant no rights to the names or logos below.
This policy says what you may do with them anyway.

## The marks

- The names **Fog**, **fogapp**, **Fin**, **Stream** and **Glass**, as names
  of this software and of services built on it.
- Their logos, icons and tile artwork, wherever they appear in these
  repositories.

They belong to Matthew Werner.

## What you may do without asking

- **Refer to the software by name**, truthfully: "runs on Fog", "a Glass
  client for Python", "how I set up Fin", "a fork of Stream". Articles,
  talks, tutorials, reviews and package listings that describe the software
  accurately are fine.
- **Run it under its own names**, modified or not, for yourself, your
  household, or your organisation's own use.
- **Redistribute unmodified copies** under the original names and logos,
  including packaging them for a platform (a container image, a distribution
  package), as long as you don't suggest that the result is official or
  endorsed.
- **Say where a fork came from**: "based on Fin" in its README or about page.

## What needs a different name

- **A modified version you distribute or offer to others** needs its own name
  and its own artwork, neither confusingly similar to these marks. The
  licenses let you make it; this policy only asks that people can tell it
  apart from the original.
- **Offering the software to others as a service or product under these
  names**, modified or not ("Fog Hosting", "Managed Glass", "Fin Cloud"),
  needs written permission.
- **Don't put the marks in your company, product or domain name**, or in an
  app-store listing for something that isn't this software.
- **Don't suggest endorsement or affiliation** that doesn't exist.

## Third-party marks

The catalogs list other projects' apps (Jellyfin, Immich, Gitea and the rest).
Their names, logos and screenshots belong to those projects and are used only
to identify them. This policy says nothing about them.

## Asking

If you want to do something this page doesn't allow, or aren't sure, open an
issue in the repository concerned and ask. Permission is usually easy to give
when people won't be confused about who made what.
